About · Profile Queenstown, NZ → Melbourne 2026

Sam Campbell, in long form.

I'm a final-year Bachelor of Fashion Marketing student at Collarts, with six years of high-end retail behind me and an independent brand project on the go. Relocating from Queenstown to Melbourne in early 2026.

Background

I came up working the floor at Seletti Concept Store in Arrowtown, a multi-label boutique stocking Ganni, Isabel Marant, Axel Arigato and Maison Margiela. Six years there taught me how a high-end brand environment actually runs: visual merchandising, customer relationships, the difference between a campaign and a vibe.

One of the highlights was coordinating and styling a fashion show at the Christchurch Porsche showroom, managing 20+ models, outfits, and event logistics. I've also worked in front of the camera as a model in store campaigns, newsletters and social content, which gave me an unusual second perspective: how the work feels from the talent side, not just the producer side.

Alongside that, I'm developing Madeleine Multi-Wear (MMW), an independent fashion brand project I've been building from concept to identity since 2023. It's where I'm applying what I'm learning at Collarts directly to a real brand context: positioning, visual direction, marketing planning.

What I'm looking for

Graduate roles in brand styling, creative direction, and fashion marketing, particularly with independent fashion houses, multi-label boutiques, and the small creative studios that work with them. Relocating to Melbourne or Sydney in early 2026.
